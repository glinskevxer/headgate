# changelog

## 1.0.0

The first version a district could run a year on end to end: build the canal,
take orders, administer a call, close the season, restate it when a weir turns
out to have been reading high, and put the packet in front of the board.

- delivery records carry their provenance, and only a measured one is evidence
- calls are refused as futile when shutting juniors would not deliver in time
- the ditch loss is charged out under whichever rule the board adopted
- a short year is rationed with an allotment that is never clawed back
- storage, leases, wells and transfers all read the same delivery record
- the book is append only: reversals and restatements, never an edit
- `docs/commands.md` and `docs/schema.md` are generated and checked in the build
